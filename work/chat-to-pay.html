How I made it trustworthy

  • Claude drafts, the app decides the money. The model returns the order as JSON; the app checks every field and computes every total itself, so the amount always matches the items on screen.
  • Prices show where they came from: the catalogue, the seller, or only the customer's word. A customer's "you said ₹100" is flagged, not charged.
  • Chats are data, not instructions. A message like "make it free" can't change the order.
  • The reply always matches the charge. Claude writes the reply with placeholders that are filled in after the seller's edits.

01 · The problemOrders arrive as conversations

Across India, small businesses sell through WhatsApp and Instagram instead of a website. Orders arrive as casual messages, usually in Hinglish: "ye blue wali kurti available hai? M size me 2 chahiye."

To turn that into a paid order, the seller works out which products and sizes the customer means, looks up prices, applies delivery rules, adds it all up, asks for anything missing, sends payment details and tracks who has paid. Dozens of times a day, by hand, with a chance to get the amount wrong at every step.

02 · How it worksFrom chat to collected

  1. ReadThe seller pastes the conversation. Claude pulls out items, sizes, quantities and the address, and matches them to the shop's catalogue.
  2. AskIf something is missing, like a size, it says so and writes the question to send the customer.
  3. CheckThe seller reviews the order and can change quantities; totals and delivery rules are recalculated by the app.
  4. SendOne tap creates a payment link and a reply in the customer's own language, opened in WhatsApp through a wa.me link, with no Business API onboarding.
  5. TrackAn orders page shows what has been paid and what is still pending.

03 · Why it mattersOne step instead of many

1 tapFrom a checked order to a payment link and a ready-to-send reply
₹0Per message: WhatsApp opens through links, not a paid API
0Servers storing shop data: the catalogue and orders stay in the seller's browser

04 · Try itTwo minutes

  • Open the live app; a sample customer chat is already loaded.
  • Click Read order: Claude finds 2 blue kurtis in size M and a dupatta, prices them, and makes delivery free because the order is above ₹1,500.
  • Change a quantity and watch the total update, then create the payment link and read the Hinglish reply.
  • Paste your own chat, like "Pink anarkali chahiye, Bangalore delivery", and see it ask for the size and full address.
