Signals from real nights.

Deep Learning Engineer Intern

Temple (Eternal)

The problem

Temple's app tells people when they fell asleep and when they woke, using a wearable that sits on the temple, beside the eye, and only its PPG and accelerometer. It has to hold up on real nights, on real hardware.

What shipped

A brief-movement detector on accelerometer data that catches short movements during sleep. I built it with the product team and it runs in the production app. I also made the existing onset/offset detection more robust.

What I built

An ML path to onset and offset: CNN-LSTM hybrids benchmarked against threshold baselines, XGBoost, LSTM and Transformer models on epoch accuracy, F1 and boundary error, with the preprocessing behind them: artifact rejection, band-pass filtering and epoch features.

IEEE · CIEES 2025 · Ruse, BulgariaPeer-reviewed

Benchmarking Machine Learning Techniques in Under-Resourced Contexts: Analysis of Public Perceptions of Government Policies from South Karnataka Reddit Discourse

Aman Tripathi, with Varnica Sharma & Kavitha Mahesh

Abstract. We gauge public sentiment on government policies in South Karnataka from 1,196 Reddit comments, comparing SVM, Decision Tree, Naïve Bayes, KNN and Random Forest with DistilBERT when training data is very scarce. K-Means clustering and BERTopic surface the themes people discuss most: healthcare infrastructure, government accountability and gaps in implementation. K-Means produced clearly separated, keyword-driven themes (94.6% accuracy), and the study sets the first benchmark for sentiment and topic classification in this regional setting.

05 About

Hello, I'm Aman.

I build machine learning for the signals bodies give off, and I care most about the step everyone skips: making the signal trustworthy before anything is trained on it.

I studied computer science, statistics and mathematics at Chandigarh University, then an M.Sc. in Data Science at Manipal. I worked on sleep detection at Temple, and I release tools other researchers can install and check: GlucoTrace on PyPI, open pipelines for mobile EEG and wrist PPG, and fixes for NeuroKit2.

I like problems where the signal is messy, the hardware is real, and the result has to be measured properly.
