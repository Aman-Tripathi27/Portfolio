08Research · IEEE CIEES 2025

Benchmarking ML in under-resourced contexts

A peer-reviewed paper on reading public opinion about government policy in South Karnataka from Reddit, when there is very little labelled data. Published at IEEE CIEES 2025 (Ruse, Bulgaria), with Varnica Sharma and Kavitha Mahesh.

  • 1,196 Reddit comments analysed
  • 5 classical models vs. DistilBERT for sentiment, plus K-Means and BERTopic for themes
  • 94.6% K-Means cluster accuracy; the first benchmark for this regional setting
