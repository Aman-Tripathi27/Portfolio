02 Model

Pick what the problem needs.

Not the biggest model, the right one. Sometimes that's a Transformer, sometimes a graph network or an LLM, and sometimes no training at all. The choice comes from the data and the benchmark, not from fashion.

for GlucoTrace

Transformer

Learns the shape of a whole glucose day in 511k parameters.

for Temple

CNN-LSTM

Convolutions read local signal shape; the LSTM carries the whole night.

for GNN-DDI

Graph autoencoder

Molecules are graphs, so the model learns on graphs.

for Chat-to-Pay

LLM, structured output

Claude reads the chat and returns JSON. The app checks it and does the maths.

for Wrist PPG

No training at all

The watch's own motion sensors cancel the noise. 0.34 s per recording.

for VMD forecasting

XGBoost

Chosen by evidence: the best of 8 models on the benchmark.

03 Evaluate

Then I try to prove it wrong.

A score is only worth the test behind it. So I lock the test set before training, test on people the model has never seen, and break things on purpose to see what still holds.
